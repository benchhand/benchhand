# Small mechanism: latch lever

Design a hand-operated latch lever on a base bracket: a conventional small mechanism with a bought pin and retaining ring.

- A base bracket screws to a panel with two M4 screws. It carries a lever between two cheeks on a pivot pin.
- The pivot is a Ø6 mm steel pin, 30 mm long, with a groove at each end for a DIN 6799 retaining ring, size 5 (for 6 to 8 mm shafts). A purchased grooved pin is acceptable.
- The lever is about 80 mm long from the pivot to its handle end, and 8 mm thick where it sits between the cheeks.
- Travel: the lever turns from 0° (latched, lying along the bracket) to 75° (open). Hard stops on the bracket end the travel at both ends.
- Clearance: 0.3 mm axial gap between the lever and each cheek; the lever runs on the pin with a clearance fit. At least 1 mm clearance between the lever and the bracket everywhere in its travel, except at the stops.
- A user pushes the handle end with up to 30 N.
- The bracket and the lever print in PETG on our Prusa MK4 (250 × 210 × 220 mm, 0.4 mm nozzle). The pin hole in the lever is reamed after printing.
- Indoor use; we need 4.
