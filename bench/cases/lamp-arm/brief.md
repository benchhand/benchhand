# Lamp arm

A desk lamp arm with three revolute joints: a base plate with a pivot boss, a lower link, an upper link and a lamp head. The design record already exists in `hw/` (it started as `examples/lamp-arm`).

- The head pivots at least 45° each way relative to the upper link. The elbow folds from 0° to -120°. The base pivot turns from -10° to 80°.
- The structure carries a 0.8 kg head at full horizontal reach.
- The base fastens to a work surface with two M6 fasteners.
- The base and head are printed in PETG by an FDM bureau; the two links are machined from 6061-T6 aluminium by a CNC bureau. The pivots run on stainless shoulder pins made to drawing, retained by DIN 471 rings, with brass bushings in the printed pivots.
- Only the mechanical parts are in scope; the lampholder and wiring are not.
