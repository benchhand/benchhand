# Wall bracket

Design a wall-mounted shelf bracket for our workshop: one custom-made bracket part, fixed with bought fasteners.

- The bracket carries one end of a shelf board: 18 mm plywood, 250 mm deep, sitting on top of the bracket.
- Tip load: the bracket must carry a static 200 N load at the shelf's front edge, 250 mm from the wall face, plus about 15 N of shelf weight. Tip deflection under the 200 N load must stay below 2 mm.
- It mounts to a vertical steel wall rail. The rail face is 40 mm wide and 2 mm thick, with M6 rivet nuts installed at a 32 mm vertical pitch. Two ISO 4762 M6 socket head cap screws hold the bracket to the rail.
- Two 4 × 16 mm countersunk wood screws, from below through the bracket's top flange, hold the shelf board.
- The bracket is CNC machined from 6061-T6 aluminium by an outsourced bureau. We need 20 of them.
- Indoor, dry workshop at room temperature.
