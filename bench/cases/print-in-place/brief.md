# Print-in-place hinged box

Design a small hinged box that prints in one piece, hinge included, with no assembly.

- Outside size about 60 × 40 × 30 mm closed: a base tray 22 mm tall and a lid 8 mm tall.
- The lid joins the base along one 60 mm edge with a print-in-place hinge: printed knuckles on both parts around a printed pin, formed in the same print.
- Hinge clearance: 0.3 mm radial clearance between the pin and each knuckle bore, and 0.3 mm axial gap between neighbouring knuckles. The hinge must turn freely after printing with no support inside it.
- The lid opens from 0° (closed) to 110°, where a stop on the hinge stops it. Closed, the lid sits on the base rim.
- Walls 1.6 mm. Prints in PLA on our Prusa MK4 (250 × 210 × 220 mm build volume, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers), in the orientation that keeps the hinge axis parallel to the bed.
- It holds small parts on a desk; we will print 5.
