# FDM enclosure

Design a two-part 3D-printed enclosure, a housing and a lid, for a small electronics board.

- The PCB is 80 × 50 × 1.6 mm with four Ø3.2 mm mounting holes on a 72 × 42 mm rectangle. Components reach 15 mm above the board's top face and 3 mm below its bottom face. Treat the PCB with its component envelope as one bought part.
- One USB-C receptacle sits at the middle of one 50 mm board edge, its opening 9 × 3.5 mm, centred 3 mm above the board's top face. The housing needs a matching opening.
- The PCB screws onto four bosses in the housing with M2.5 × 6 mm ISO 7380 button head screws into M2.5 brass heat-set inserts.
- The lid fastens to the housing with four M3 × 8 mm ISO 7380 button head screws into M3 brass heat-set inserts in the housing's corner bosses.
- Keep at least 2 mm clearance between the component envelope and every inside wall and the lid.
- Both parts print in PETG on our Bambu Lab X1C (256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume, 0.4 mm nozzle). Walls at least 2 mm.
- Indoor desk use. We need 10.
